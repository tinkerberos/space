I really enjoyed this little read. I've never read anything from Steinbeck before, and I know this is a classic, but the writing is great. Great combination of humor and  emotion. Like the dejection in the room when {{< spoiler >}} old man Candy foreits his dog to Carlson{{< /spoiler >}}. You can feel the walls swelling over. The description(s) of the their dream was also great. {{< spoiler >}} Lennie was finally able to live out their dream, uninterrupted. {{< /spoiler >}}

What makes this read even better is I just finished playing Red Dead Redepemption 2, I kept [hearing](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7CxNKiBfZEQ) Arthur Morgan's voice when George says "sure". I found it pretty amusing.

> "Sure," said George. "All kind's a vegetables in the garden" (54)

Also, I learned what those hobo sticks are called.
